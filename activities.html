Local Adventures & Events

One of the best things about living in Cary and Morrisville is all the green space. Here are two of the best free parks to explore, plus a fun seasonal spot!

Lake Crabtree County Park

Located right between Morrisville and Cary, Lake Crabtree County Park has beautiful walking and biking trails, an observation tower, and quiet spots to sit by the water. It is one of my favorite places to clear my head after a busy week of classes.

  • Cost: Free admission and free parking
  • Activities: Walking trails, biking, picnics, and watching the sunset
  • Website: Lake Crabtree Park Info

Downtown Cary Park

The new Downtown Cary Park is a central hub for walking, relaxing on swings, and attending free community events like outdoor movie nights and live music. It is always lively, safe, and full of students and young professionals hanging out.

  • Cost: Free entry
  • Activities: Skywalk bridges, gardens, food trucks, and community events
  • Website: Downtown Cary Park Calendar

Seasonal Feature: Phillips Farms of Cary

If you are an international student or newcomer who wants to experience traditional American fall festivities, Phillips Farms is the place to go! Located near the Cary/Apex border, they have a huge corn maze, pumpkin patch, wagon rides, and fresh apple cider donuts.

  • Season: Fall (September through November)
  • Highlights: Corn maze, pumpkin harvest, and fall photo spots
  • Website: Phillips Farms Official Site
