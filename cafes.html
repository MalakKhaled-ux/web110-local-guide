Study Spots & Sips

Finding a good place to study or meet new people makes a huge difference when you are new to town. Here are my favorite local spots with great drinks and a welcoming vibe.

Purple & Pearl

Purple & Pearl is a super aesthetic and cozy cafe that is great for hanging out with friends or getting some reading done. They have specialty teas, lattes, and really pretty desserts.

  • Best for: Aesthetic vibes, sweet treats, and casual study sessions
  • What to try: Specialty milk teas, lattes, and pastries
  • Price range: Around $6 to $9

Local Vietnamese Coffee Spots (Morrisville)

Morrisville has amazing diverse cafes where you can get authentic Vietnamese iced coffee (made with strong dark roast coffee and sweet condensed milk). Spots like Paris Banh Mi and local pho & coffee shops have big tables, fast Wi-Fi, and strong drinks that will keep you awake through any study session.

  • Best for: Long laptop study sessions and strong caffeine
  • What to try: Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Ca Phe Sua Da) and a crispy Banh Mi sandwich
  • Price range: $5 to $8
