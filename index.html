Welcome to the Area!

Moving to a new town or starting fresh as an international student can feel overwhelming. I live right here in the Cary and Morrisville area, and I created this guide to share my favorite cozy, safe, and budget-friendly spots to study, hang out, and explore nature.

Whether you need strong coffee and free Wi-Fi for homework or a free park to walk around on the weekend, you can find it here without spending a ton of money.

Quick Tips for Newcomers

  • Free Bus Rides: GoCary buses are free to ride and help you get around Cary and Morrisville easily.
  • Best Study Times: Cafes get busy on Saturday and Sunday afternoons, so try going on weekday mornings if you need a plug outlet.
  • Park Wi-Fi: Downtown Cary Park has free public Wi-Fi if you want to do homework outside on a sunny day!
